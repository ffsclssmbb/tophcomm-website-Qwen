## 🎯 Design System Changes

### Color Palette
- **Background**: Pure black `#000000` (was `#0d0d0d`)
- **Text**: Pure white `#ffffff`
- **Muted**: `#9a9a9a` (was `#a1a1aa`)
- **Stat**: `#d8d8d8`
- **Border**: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.16)`

### Typography
- **Primary**: Inter (variable weight 100-900)
- **Accent**: Instrument Serif (italic) for emphasis words
- **Headlines**: Inter 500 with tight tracking `-0.045em`
- **Emphasis**: Instrument Serif italic with muted color `#9a9a9a`

### Visual Effects
- ✅ **Grain texture overlay** (SVG noise at 8% opacity, overlay blend mode)
- ✅ **Liquid-glass buttons** with shine animation
- ✅ **Liquid-metal nav pills** with gradient backgrounds
- ✅ **Glass cards** with backdrop blur
- ✅ **Entrance animations** (scale, soft, mask, pop, btn, side, stat)

### Button Styles
1. **Solid**: White gradient with glow on hover
2. **Ghost**: Frosted glass with backdrop blur
3. **Nav Pill**: Liquid metal with shine sweep
