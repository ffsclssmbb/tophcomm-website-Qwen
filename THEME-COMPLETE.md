## 🎨 Design System

### Colors
- **Background**: Pure black `#000000`
- **Text**: Pure white `#ffffff`
- **Muted**: `#9a9a9a`
- **Stat**: `#d8d8d8`
- **Border**: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.16)`

### Typography
- **Primary**: Inter (variable weight 100-900)
- **Accent**: Instrument Serif (italic for emphasis)
- **Headlines**: Inter 500, tracking `-0.045em`
- **Emphasis**: Instrument Serif italic, color `#9a9a9a`

### Visual Effects
- ✅ **Grain texture overlay** (SVG noise, 8% opacity, overlay blend)
- ✅ **Liquid-glass buttons** with shine animation
- ✅ **Liquid-metal nav pills** with gradient backgrounds
- ✅ **Glass cards** with backdrop blur
- ✅ **Entrance animations** (scale, soft, mask, pop, btn, side, stat)

### Button Styles
1. **Solid**: White gradient → hover glow effect
2. **Ghost**: Frosted glass with backdrop blur → hover border glow
3. **Nav Pill**: Liquid metal with shine sweep on hover
